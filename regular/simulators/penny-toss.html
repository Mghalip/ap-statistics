How the simulation works: each landing point is generated with the same random error left–right as forward–back (a bivariate normal scatter around the centre), and the recorded distance is √(x² + y²). That is why the distances pile up near the centre with a long right tail — a Rayleigh-shaped, right-skewed distribution, so the mean gets pulled above the median by the occasional wild toss.
