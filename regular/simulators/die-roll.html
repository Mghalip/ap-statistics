Roll a Die, Record the Result

A simulation of one fair six-sided die. Every face — 1 through 6 — is equally likely, so the distribution is flat (uniform) with theoretical mean 3.5. Roll a few times and the results look random; roll a few thousand times and the pattern settles down.

What to watch: with only a handful of rolls, one face can easily lead by luck. As the total grows into the hundreds and thousands, each relative frequency drifts toward 1⁄6 and the sample mean drifts toward 3.5. That settling-down is the Law of Large Numbers — the reason a simulation can stand in for theory.
Fair die · P(each face) = 1⁄6 · Mean = Median = 3.5 · Uniform distribution
